# Community conduct / 社区行为准则

Help others contribute effectively. Discuss proposals and evidence with respect, explain disagreements, and make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience. This applies to issues, PRs, reviews, comments and project-related conversations.

帮助他人有效参与贡献。尊重地讨论提案与依据，说明分歧，包容不同背景和经验水平。本准则适用于 issue、PR、审阅、评论及项目相关交流。

Do not harass, threaten, insult or discriminate against participants. Do not publish someone else's personal information, pressure them for credentials, or post private project material in public. Critique the work rather than attacking its author.

不得骚扰、威胁、侮辱或歧视参与者。不得公开他人个人信息、逼迫他人提供凭据，或公开私有项目材料。评价工作本身，不攻击作者。

Keep contributions within the repository's stated scope. Do not use issues or PRs for spam, impersonation, credential collection or unauthorized access. Respect decisions to stop an interaction and the separation between public onboarding and private development.

贡献应符合仓库声明的范围。不得利用 issue 或 PR 发布垃圾信息、冒充他人、收集凭据或进行未授权访问。尊重停止互动的决定，遵守公开接入与私有开发之间的边界。

For a concern, use a private contact method published by the [repository owner](https://github.com/DapengFeng), if available. Otherwise, ask for a private contact channel in a public issue without naming affected people or posting sensitive details. Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit interactions or remove project access, and should explain the reason to the affected participant when it is safe to do so.

如有疑虑，使用[仓库所有者](https://github.com/DapengFeng)公布的私密联系方式（若有）。否则，可在公开 issue 中请求私密联系渠道，但不要点名受影响人员或发布敏感细节。维护者可以移除有害内容、限制互动或移除项目访问权限，并应在安全可行时向相关参与者说明原因。

Moderation and repository access are separate from the automated CLA check. A valid signature does not excuse misconduct; withdrawing a signature does not automatically remove already accepted access. The [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) owns the technical access workflow.

社区管理和仓库权限与自动 CLA 检查分别处理。有效签署不能豁免不当行为；撤回签署也不会自动移除已经接受的访问权限。技术接入流程由[贡献指南](CONTRIBUTING.md)统一说明。
